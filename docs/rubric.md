# Review rubric

Score each response against its prompt. Rate each response independently and record evidence before comparing variants.

| Dimension | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Correct facts or calculations, complete extraction, and claims supported by the supplied context. |
| Instruction following | Required format, ordering, length, output constraints, and requested content. |
| Clarity | Understandable wording and an answer that a reader can use. |

Use **5** for fully meeting the dimension, **4** for a minor issue, **3** for a material but partial issue, **2** for a major issue, and **1** when the response fails the dimension completely.

Use a **fail** verdict when a material accuracy issue, a required-format violation, or unclear wording prevents the answer from satisfying the task. Otherwise use **pass**. Record the exact wording, calculation, or requirement behind the decision. The verdict is entered independently of the numeric scores so that a critical error cannot be averaged away.

For the synthetic demo, two reviewers intentionally disagree on the name-only extraction and the unsupported stipend approval. The aggregation policy flags both for follow-up.
